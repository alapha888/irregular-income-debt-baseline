# Irregular-Income Debt Baseline Calculator

A free single-page tool for freelancers, contractors, and gig workers paying off
debt on irregular income. No sign-up; all math runs in your browser.

**Live calculator:** https://alapha888.github.io/irregular-income-debt-baseline/

## The idea

A budget built on average income breaks in a low month. This calculator sizes
everything to your **lowest month** (the baseline), then treats a good month as a
bonus to allocate — never as the plan.

The three-tier rule:

1. **Buffer under 1 month of essentials** — pay minimums only; every spare dollar
   goes to the buffer first. A low month with no buffer is how people re-borrow
   to pay debt.
2. **Buffer at 1 month** — minimums, then all extra to one target debt
   (Snowball: smallest balance; Avalanche: highest APR).
3. **Buffer at 3 months** — throw everything above essentials + minimums at the
   target debt.

The debt-free estimate assumes every future month equals your lowest month —
deliberately conservative. Good months finish sooner.

## What it is not

A baseline planner, not financial advice. It does not know your loan terms, fees,
penalty rates, or tax situation. It assumes fixed APRs and no new borrowing.
